# Possible applied cases — research ideas, not yet identified causal designs

## Indonesian education and labor outcomes
- Vocational (SMK) versus general (SMA) upper secondary schooling and higher-education progression.
- Poverty and household cash-transfer receipt as dimensions of heterogeneity.
- Employment and wages after completing school.

**Identification audit:** school-track selection, baseline confounding, household resources, timing, attrition, interference, positivity/overlap and sampling design.

A first study should produce descriptions, timing diagrams, a DAG and overlap checks. DML cannot eliminate unmeasured confounding or repair a flawed design.

## Hypothetical RAPSEM application
Randomized educational intervention -> latent motivation -> academic performance, with measured baseline moderators of the intervention's effect on motivation.

This is an *illustrative design*, not an assertion that such data exist. Randomization, moderator interactions, exclusion restrictions and measurement assumptions require careful analysis.

## Policy-learning extensions
Later consider IV encouragement rules, causal forests and heterogeneous treatment effects. Keep these in separate modules until their assumptions and estimands are clear.
